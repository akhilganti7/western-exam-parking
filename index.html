      Pit-stop rules (aka how not to get ticketed)

      Weekdays: always payCampus visitor parking is paid 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday. Most spots are $1.50 per half hour. Daily maxes change by lot.
      Weekends: freeVisitor lots are free on Saturday and Sunday, unless a special event is on.
      What's a Honk zone?Every paid visitor lot has a 4-digit zone number on its signs. Open the Honk Mobile app (or honkmobile.com), type that zone, pick your time and pay. No ticket on the dash: Western checks your plate.
      Your plate is your ticketWestern scans licence plates. Double-check your plate in Honk Mobile.
      Credit card onlyNo tap or debit at gates. Coins only at Pay & Display machines. Honk Mobile works anywhere with a zone code.
      "After 4 pm" lotsSome permit lots only open to visitors 4 pm to 7 am. Park there for a morning exam and you get a ticket.
      EV spotsOnly for EVs that are plugged in. You still pay visitor parking while charging. The ChargePoint app shows which chargers work.
      Gated lotsParked overnight in a gated visitor lot? Leave by 7 am.
      City streets (Nov 1 – Apr 30)No street parking 3 to 5 a.m. without an overnight pass, and 12 hours max at a time.
